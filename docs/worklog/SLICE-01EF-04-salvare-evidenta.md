# SLICE-01EF-04 — «Contari»: the save of the evidence through the posting service

## What changed and why
**Salvează evidențierea** books the evidence of the ticked invoices (Access `SaveEFactura`, evidence half, without the FOREXE part). `POST /api/efactura/contari/salveaza` (role Contabil, checked on the server) runs the same calculation as the page, in ONE transaction with the invoices locked (`FOR UPDATE`), then writes through the **posting service** (`PYTHON/routes/avacont/introducere_date/operatii/postare.py`, the only code that writes `TZ_NoteC`, `TZ_Documente`, `TZ_Oper`, `TZ_Debit`, `TZ_Credit`):
- one **accounting note** (`NumarNC` = highest of the subunit and year + 1);
- per invoice with something left: a **document** (`Fel` E, `Auto` 1, number = digits of the last word of the invoice number) and an **operation** from the single template line of the form (journal and explanation of the template, `Tip` E, `Angajament` = `Propunere` = `PA` of the line, `Chelt`, `NrAng` = next of the subunit and year), with its **debit and credit** (the same amount, negative for a credit note; accounts found in the subunit's chart from the base account, the analytic and the classification; partner by the line's mode NIMIC / OPERATIE / FIX);
- one row of `EF_PrimiteOperatii` per operation (ties the operation to the invoice; its key is taken as highest + 1 because the column has no auto-increment).
The subunit is the active one with the sector+source of the classification (the default among several). A date in a closed month (document `Fel` I of the subunit and year) moves to the 1st of the next month; a date of an earlier year to 1 January of the work year (shown on the page as an info warning and in the grid). The warnings that need a confirmation travel as codes in `confirmari`; without them the server answers 409 `CONFIRMARE_NECESARA` with their texts. Any warning of kind «eroare» refuses the save.

Client: button **Salvează evidențierea** (right) beside **Renunță** (left, button-side rule); enabled when the role, the classification, the evidence template allow and nothing blocks; shows the confirmations together (Yes/No), then the result («nota contabilă N, n operații»), takes the ticks off and reads the list again.

## Unverified / deferred
- The whole write path (SQL, foreign keys, number allocation under lock, `FOR UPDATE` on the invoices) is untested.
- A template has AT MOST ONE line per form (the window allows no more; operator, 02.11.2026), or one line valid for every form, used only when no line of the form exists. The save takes that line; a template without a line for the form is refused with a message (the page shows it before).
- **Notes (operator, 02.11.2026): a combo with the existing notes AND the option to type a new number** (`cmbNota`, editable, not limited to the list). The server returns the notes of the subunit and year and the next number (`note`); `numarNC` in the save: an existing number takes the new documents, an unused number makes a note with that number, none = the next number; a text that is not a positive whole number is refused on the page. This is the rule for the notes in every window (memory `accounting-note-combo-everywhere`).
- A document may be added to a note of another year only if the dates fall in the work year; a date that leaves the work year (December closing → January) refuses the save with `AN_DIFERIT`.
- The payment (documents, operations, order of payment, the link with the ORD) is 01EF-05 and AV02-02; nothing about the payment is saved.
- **The subunit is the application's (operator, 02.11.2026, correcting the first version).** K-BOT does not use it, but the accounting operations and the other Avacont operations (notes, orders of payment, the templates in use) belong to a subunit. The selector of the title bar (`KBotCaptionBar.SelectorSubunit`) stands BEFORE «Sursa/Sector» and is drawn AT ANY TIME, on any page, when the source in force (the title bar's) has two or more active subunits. `KbotForm` (`KbotForm.Subunit.vb`) reads the subunits once per database (`GetSubunitatiAsync`), follows `SessionContext.PeriodChanged`, keeps the subunit in force (`CurrentSubunitId`) and raises `SubunitPicked`; the selector has already moved when the pages hear it. `ISubunitHost` (KBot.EFactura) and `IPlanContHost` carry `SubunitPicked`, `CurrentSubunitId` and `SetSubunitKey` (the page puts the selector back when it cannot follow a pick); `SetSubunitChoices` / `SetSubunitShown` are gone. «Contari» sends `idSubunitate` on every call; the server offers only the classifications of the subunit's sector+source and books in that subunit. «Plan de conturi» takes the subunit in force when it opens and when it comes back on screen, and ignores a pick while hidden. Not seen on screen.
