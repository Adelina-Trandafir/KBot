## What changed and why

- **Server** `PYTHON/routes/avacont/introducere_date/operatii/op9.py`: `GET /api/avacont/operatii-diverse/context` (notes of the subunit and year, next note number, last closing) and `POST /api/avacont/operatii-diverse/salveaza`. One transaction: for every template line with an amount other than zero, ONE document (`Fel = A`, number / kind / date typed by the operator) and ONE operation (`Tip = A`, one debit and one credit line) through the posting service. The accounts, the partner mode and the flags are read from the template ON THE SERVER; the client sends only the line ids and the amounts. Refused: a template that is not of type A or not of the subunit, a date outside the work year or on / before the last closing (the Access rule, not moved), a missing partner when a side says «the partner of the operation», no line with an amount, an account missing from the chart of the subunit.
- **Client**: domain `src/KBot.Domain/Avacont/IntroducereDate/Operatii/OperatiiInfo.vb`; API `src/KBot.Api/Avacont/IntroducereDate/Operatii/IOperatiiApi.vb` + `ApiClient.Operatii.vb`; the page `src/KBot.App/Avacont/IntroducereDate/Operatii/OperatiiDiversePage.vb` (+ Designer): searchable list of the templates of type A (subunit and sector+source of the title bar), header (note, number, kind, date, partner, amount), table of lines with the amount editable per line, «Salveaza» / «Renunta».
- The window `OperatiiSabloaneForm` (modal, vertical bar, pattern of `SabloaneForm`), opened from the ribbon button **Date › Operatii** (`operatii_sabloane`, `KbotForm.Operatii.vb`).
- **Left out on purpose:** the Access FX / ALG part (commitment contract, account 8067), by the operator's rule of 31.10.2026.
