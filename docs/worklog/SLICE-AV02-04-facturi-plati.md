## Decisions taken while building (they differ from the plan where said)

- **Its own page, not the page «Contari» of FacturiCumparare.** The plan said to reuse `FacturiCumparareContarePage` without the tree. That page and its server calculation are keyed by `EF_Primite` (an invoice = an ANAF message), so reusing it would have meant rewriting both. `FacturiPlatiPage` is a stand-alone view (layout below) over a server flow of its own that shares the SERVICES: `postare.py`, `plati.py` (`posteaza_ordin`, `aloca_pe_operatii`, numbering, lock, delete) and the helpers of `primite_contari.py` (templates, classifications, codes, the text of the order). `primite_plata.posteaza` now uses the same `plati.posteaza_ordin`.
- **A typed invoice needs no new table.** It is the evidence itself: a document (`Fel = E`) and an operation (`Tip = E`) from the evidence template, with the partner on the credit side. A payment is allocated on that evidence operation in `TZ_PlatiFacturi.IdEvidentiere` (`IdPrimita` stays NULL, as the DDL of AV02-02 already says). The rest to pay = the credit lines of the partner on the operation − the sum of the allocations on it (`plati.valori_pe_operatii`). So the same list serves the invoices typed here and any evidence of the partner already in the books.
- **«Anul anterior»** lists the evidence operations of the previous year that THE DATABASE holds (notes of year − 1). The previous year before the port is not in the unit database, so for migrated history the list stays empty until the opening balances / the history of the previous year are ported (`IdEvidentiereAnterior` of the DDL is for that, unused here).
- The reception check and the tick «Protectie contari Plati/Receptii» do NOT apply here: a typed invoice has no reception link.

## Layout of the page (operator's sketch, 10.11.2026; machete type M = classification + partner on one side)

`FacturiPlatiPage` was redesigned from the operator's drawing; nothing is shared with the E-Factura pages (the operator: «NU mai refolosim EFACTURA»; the one exception left is the helper class `OpExplicatie`, see Unverified).
- **Top:** Clasificatia (over the left column) | Partenerul | Cod client | Cod bancar. The note and the date of the order are NOT on top any more: they are cells of the payment row.
- **Middle:** left = «Documente evidentiere» (evidence templates); right = a horizontal nav list (Facturi noi / Facturi an curent / Facturi an anterior) over ONE view = three stand-alone grids, one shown at a time. The grid of new invoices has the columns number, date, kind, **«Nota contabila» (MANDATORY on every invoice, a combo: an existing note or a typed new number)**, value, to pay, and the standard DGV delete column (`ButtonKind.Delete`). It always ends with a blank row to type in (no delete glyph on it; a new blank row follows once it is used and starts in the note of the invoice just typed; the blank row is never saved).
- **Bottom:** left = «Documente plata» (payment templates); right = ONE payment grid whose first row is the payment being made (provisional O.P. number, date, note as a combo, explanation, value paid now) and whose other rows are the orders of the partner in the year, read-only (`CellFormatting.ReadOnlyCell`), each with the standard delete column (the payment row has none; an order that is blocked answers with the reason instead of deleting). No «Stare» column.
- **Very low:** status line + «Renunta», «Salveaza». **NO row-delete buttons anywhere on the page** (operator, 10.11.2026: «NICIODATA PE NICIUN FORM»): «Adauga factura», «Sterge randul» and «Sterge plata» do not exist; deleting is the DGV delete column.

## What changed

- **Server** `PYTHON/routes/avacont/introducere_date/operatii/facturi_plati.py`: `GET /api/avacont/facturi-plati/context` (notes, partners, classifications with their templates), `GET .../deschise` (unpaid invoices of the partner, current and previous year; the orders of the year with their lock; the provisional number of the next order; the account), `POST .../salveaza` (typed invoices + payment with its order, ONE transaction), `DELETE .../plata/<idop>` (an unlocked order with its payment). The real order number comes back in `plata.nrop`.
- **Shared code**: `plati.py` gets `posteaza_ordin`, `aloca_pe_operatii`, `rest_pe_operatii` / `valori_pe_operatii`, `id_partener_linie`; `primite_plata.py` calls `posteaza_ordin`.
- **Client**: `FacturiPlatiPage` (+ Designer, generated) and the same window `OperatiiSabloaneForm` (page «Facturi si plati», read the first time it is shown). The class `OpExplicatie` (the ASCII text of the order, `KBot.EFactura`) became `Public` so the page can reuse it.
